J-Way Rewards — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

J-Way Rewards ("the app") is a casual rewards app. This policy describes what data the app collects and how it is used.

What data is collected

  • Advertising ID (AdMob): The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and related usage data to show and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • YouTube embeds: The Watch & Earn tab loads video content from YouTube (a Google service). Interacting with the embedded player is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Pollfish surveys: The Surveys tab uses the Pollfish survey platform. If you complete a survey, Pollfish may collect your survey responses and limited device/usage data per its own privacy policy.

What is NOT collected in this MVP

  • No accounts. The app does not require sign-up or login.
  • No names, emails, or contact details.
  • Points and progress are stored on-device in the app's local storage (localStorage). They are never uploaded to our servers and are deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data described above is collected by the third-party services (Google/AdMob, Google/YouTube, Pollfish) under their own policies when you use the corresponding features.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.
